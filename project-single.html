Project Overview

Valcore Industries managed the full construction lifecycle for this 12-story downtown commercial office complex — from pre-construction planning and contractor delegation through final closeout and tenant handover.

The project encompassed 280,000 square feet of Class A office space, delivered on schedule and within the approved budget. Valcore coordinated 40+ subcontractors, managed weekly owner reporting, and maintained zero lost-time safety incidents throughout the 18-month build.

Project Highlights

This commercial build demonstrates Valcore's ability to manage large-scale projects with multiple stakeholders, tight urban site constraints, and aggressive delivery timelines.

  • 12-story, 280,000 sq ft Class A office tower
  • 40+ vetted subcontractors coordinated by Valcore
  • Delivered on schedule across 18-month timeline
  • Zero lost-time safety incidents
  • Full budget accountability with transparent reporting
  • Seamless closeout and tenant handover
